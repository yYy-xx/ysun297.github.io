Why Practice Breads?

Working with yeasted doughs helps you understand gluten development and timing, which makes all of your baking stronger.

  • Use warm, not hot, water or milk so you don’t kill the yeast.
  • Knead until the dough is smooth and slightly springy.
  • Let the dough rise in a warm place until doubled in size.
  • Cool baked bread completely before slicing for neat pieces.

This Month’s Bread Goals

  1. Bake one basic loaf each week.
  2. Try shaping rolls or braids for fun textures.
  3. Experiment with seeds, nuts, or whole grain flour.
