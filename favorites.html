Favorite / Me

This page is a little corner of the site where I share a bit about myself and the desserts that inspire Sweet Moments Bakery.

Hi! I'm the baker behind Sweet Moments Bakery. I love gentle, cozy desserts that feel special but are still beginner-friendly. My goal is to break recipes into clear, simple steps so anyone can bake something they feel proud of—whether it's a birthday cake, weekend bread, or colorful cookies to share with friends.

Current Favorite Recipes

These are the desserts I'm the most excited about right now:

  • Chocolate Drip Cake - rich, dramatic, and perfect for celebrations. (See details on the Cakes page.)
  • Fruit Tart Basics - a bright, colorful tart that looks fancy but uses simple steps.
  • Beginner Bread Loaf - a first bread recipe with a soft inside and golden crust. (Visit the Breads page.)
  • Macarons - tiny cookie sandwiches that are fun for practicing piping and color.

Baking Goals

Some things I want to keep adding to the website:

  • Step-by-step photo or video for tricky techniques like whipping meringue and making ganache.
  • A full beginner series that moves from cookies to cakes to bread.
  • More “mistake fixing” tips so new bakers don't feel discouraged.

If you try a recipe from this site, I hope it becomes a sweet memory for you and the people you share it with.
