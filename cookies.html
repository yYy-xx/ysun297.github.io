Cookie Recipes

This page gathers our favorite cookie-style desserts. It's a space to practice piping, shaping, and decorating bite-sized sweets.

Macaron Basics

Macarons are delicate French cookies made with almond flour, egg whites, and sugar. They're crisp on the outside, chewy in the middle, and filled with buttercream, ganache, or jam.

  • Practice piping even circles on a template.
  • Let the shells rest until they form a dry skin before baking.
  • Match shells by size and sandwich gently with filling.
